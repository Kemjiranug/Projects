Customer Complaint Tracker
A Power Apps canvas application integrated with SharePoint and Power Automate to streamline customer complaint management for retail environments.
Overview
This project simulates a real-world complaint handling system for a shopping mall, built to practice Microsoft Power Platform development including app design, data management, and workflow automation.
Workflow
Customer reports issue
        ↓
Staff logs complaint via Power Apps (Log Screen)
        ↓
Data saved to SharePoint List (CustomerComplaints_EN)
        ↓
Power Automate Flow 1 triggered automatically
        ↓
   [Priority = High?]
    Yes → Urgent email sent to responsible team immediately
    No  → Standard notification email sent
        ↓
Team updates Status → "In Progress" → "Resolved"
        ↓
Power Automate Flow 2 triggered on Status change
        ↓
   [Status = Resolved?]
    Yes → Email sent back to staff who logged the complaint
        ↓
Dashboard reflects updated complaint statistics in real time
Features

Log Screen — Form to submit new complaints with category, priority, location, and contact info
Track Screen — Gallery view of all complaints with filter by status (New / In Progress / Resolved)
Dashboard — Summary cards showing total complaints by status and priority, with Pie Charts by category and status

Tech Stack

Microsoft Power Apps (Canvas App)
Microsoft SharePoint List (data source)
Microsoft Power Automate (automated email notifications)
Power Fx (app logic and filtering formulas)

Key Learnings

Connecting Power Apps to SharePoint as a data source
Using Patch() and SubmitForm() to write data
Building conditional logic with Filter() and If() in Power Fx
Creating automated flows with conditions and dynamic content in Power Automate
